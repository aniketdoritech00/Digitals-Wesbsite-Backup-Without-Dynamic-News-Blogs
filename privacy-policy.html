Privacy Policy

ERP - Enterprise Resource Planning System

Last Updated: July 22, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system collects, uses, stores, and protects information used within our organization. The ERP application is developed exclusively for internal organizational use and is intended only for authorized employees and management personnel.

Information We Collect

The ERP system may collect and maintain information including:

  • Employee details and identification information.
  • User login credentials.
  • Department and role information.
  • Customer and contract details.
  • Site and project information.
  • Expense records and reimbursement details.
  • Attendance and assignment information.
  • Approval history and audit logs.
  • System usage logs for security and troubleshooting.

Purpose of Data Collection

The collected information is used solely for official business purposes, including:

  • Managing contracts and customers.
  • Managing employee assignments.
  • Calculating operational and travel expenses.
  • Generating business reports.
  • Managing approvals and workflows.
  • Maintaining audit records.
  • Improving operational efficiency.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or destruction. Access to ERP data is restricted according to user roles and business responsibilities.

Information Sharing

Information stored within the ERP system is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties except:

  • When required by applicable law.
  • For internal auditing and compliance purposes.
  • When authorized by organizational management.

User Responsibilities

  • Keep login credentials confidential.
  • Do not share accounts with others.
  • Log out after completing work.
  • Report any suspected security incidents immediately.

Data Retention

Data is retained only for operational, legal, financial, and compliance purposes as determined by the organization's internal policies and applicable regulations.

Policy Updates

The organization reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Updated versions will be published within the ERP system.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information, please contact your system administrator or the organization's IT department.
